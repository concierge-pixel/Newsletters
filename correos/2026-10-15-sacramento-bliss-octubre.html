03 · Homeowner Tips

Seasonal care for your home

 

Fall checklist: prepare before the rain

  • Clear gutters and downspouts; make sure water flows away from the foundation.
  • Check the roof and windows for leaks or damage.
  • Replace worn weatherstripping around doors and windows.
Learn more

Small changes, a more welcoming home

  • Refresh the entryway: a clean door, new mat, a pot of greenery.
  • Improve outdoor lighting at the door and along walkways.
  • Keep walkways clear and trimmed.

04 · Buyer & Seller Corner

 

Before You Fall in Love With a Home

The right home is about more than the listing price. Before scheduling showings, understand your budget, financing options, and the additional costs of homeownership.

  • Get pre-approved and compare loan options.
  • Budget for taxes, insurance and maintenance.
Learn more
 

Is Your Home Ready for Its Next Chapter?

Thinking about moving in the next 6–12 months? A little early preparation goes a long way.

  • Start a maintenance record.
  • Identify repairs that may need attention.
  • Gather warranties and permits.
  • Talk with an agent about a pricing range before deciding when to sell.
