Moving Up To Roseville: Best Areas For Sacramento Sellers

SHARE

By Melissa Allman | Updated October 15, 2026

Moving up from Sacramento to Roseville usually means paying more for a newer or larger home about 20 miles northeast on I-80, and the price gap is smaller than many sellers expect. Redfin put Roseville's median sale price at about $630,000 for the three months ending September 2026, compared with about $508,000 in Sacramento.

That gap of roughly $122,000 is only a starting point. Roseville homes sold in a median of about 28 days in the same period, and Sacramento homes sold in about 25 days (Redfin, September 2026). Before you start touring, work out what your Sacramento sale will net and how you want to time the two transactions.

What Selling in Sacramento Costs You

Inside Sacramento city limits, the seller customarily pays the Sacramento County documentary transfer tax of $1.10 per $1,000 and the City of Sacramento transfer tax of $2.75 per $1,000. That's about $3.85 per $1,000 combined, or roughly $2,695 on a $700,000 sale. Properties outside city limits pay only the county portion.

Transfer taxes are one line in your net sheet, alongside your payoff, commission, and escrow and title fees. Ask for a written net estimate before you write an offer on a Roseville home, so you know your cash to close rather than guessing.

timing your sale and purchase

The hardest part of a move-up is sequencing. You're selling into one market and buying into another, and the two timelines rarely line up on their own.

Contingent Offers and Possession

California's standard C.A.R. residential purchase agreement includes a sale-of-buyer's-property contingency, but it isn't automatic. You select it, the seller has to accept it, and the default contingency period in the agreement is 17 days. In a market where homes sell in under a month, a contingent offer is a harder sell than a clean one.

You have other options. You can negotiate a longer close on the Roseville purchase, or ask for a possession date that gives you time to move after your Sacramento sale closes. Which path works depends on how fast your home is likely to sell, so a pricing conversation comes first.

Overlap and Bridge Costs

Plan for a stretch where you may carry two housing payments, plus moving costs and the closing costs on your purchase. Confirm the exact numbers with your lender, because loan terms and cash requirements vary by borrower.

If your Sacramento home needs work before it's listed, decide early what's worth fixing. A guide to pricing strategy for move-up sellers shows how comps and condition shape list price, and the same approach applies to a Sacramento sale.

commute, transit, and school lookups

Roseville sits on I-80, and the drive varies a lot by neighborhood, departure time, and where in Sacramento you work. Test the real drive at your actual commute hours before you commit to a home.

Commute and Transit

Roseville Transit runs express commuter routes to downtown Sacramento on weekdays during peak morning and afternoon hours, and the city's schedule page lists current routes and travel times. Routes and schedules have changed in recent years, so check the page the week you're deciding. The state workers' four-day return-to-office order that took effect July 1, 2026 makes the commute a bigger factor for many households.

School Districts and Boundary Lookups

Roseville is served by the Roseville City School District for kindergarten through eighth grade and the Roseville Joint Union High School District for high school. Assignment is based on the property address, so use each district's boundary lookup tool for the specific home you're considering. This post doesn't rate or compare schools, so rely on the districts' official pages for that information.
